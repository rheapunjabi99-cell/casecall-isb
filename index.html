Why CASECALL

Most case prep tests what you know. Interviews test how you think.

In placement season, case practice happens in a rush: casebooks, frameworks and whoever is free for a mock. That builds knowledge, but interviewers are judging something else.

What casebooks and frameworks give you

A useful start, and most students already have it.

  • Structures to start from
  • The standard case types
  • Formulas for market sizing

What interviewers actually judge

Judgement skills that only improve with reps and feedback.

  • Asking for the right data first
  • Reading an exhibit quickly and accurately
  • Changing course when the case twists
  • A clear recommendation under time pressure

CASECALL gives you reps on the second list, with honest feedback every time.

Every placement season, smart people lose case interviews they were ready for. Not because they didn’t know the frameworks. FAQ

Questions students ask

Straight answers, including what CASECALL doesn’t do.

Is this an official ISB product?

No. CASECALL is an independent student project. It isn’t affiliated with, run by or endorsed by ISB or its careers office.

Will it tell me whether I’ll get shortlisted or get an offer?

No. It gives feedback on the decisions you make in a case. It never predicts shortlists, offers or which firms you’re ready for.

Does it replace mocks with friends and seniors?

No. Practise with people for spoken delivery, pressure and presence. Use CASECALL to fix the basics first, so your mocks with people are about the hard parts.

Is it spoken or typed?

Typed for now. You read the case, type how you’d answer, and get feedback in seconds.

Are the cases real?

No. They use fictional companies and numbers, built on the case types common in consulting, product and strategy interviews.

What happens to my answers?

Answers are stored anonymously to improve CASECALL and to show the live numbers on this site. Please don’t include names or contact details in your answers.
